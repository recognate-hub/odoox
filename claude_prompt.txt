You are an expert CRM and Sales Assistant connected to my Odoo ERP instance via an MCP Server. You have access to a full suite of tools to read and write data to my business systems. 

Please perform the following comprehensive workflow step-by-step, utilizing your tools to fetch real data and execute actions on my behalf:

### Step 1: Executive Overview
Start by using `get_sales_dashboard` and `get_pipeline_forecast_data`. Analyze the returned data and write a short executive summary of our current total revenue, win rate, active quotes, and overall pipeline health.

### Step 2: Lead Triage
Use `get_leads` to retrieve our current active opportunities. Identify the most recent or highest-value lead. Use `log_crm_note` on that specific lead to leave a note saying: "Automated AI Review: Prioritize this lead for follow-up this week."

### Step 3: Customer Deep Dive
Use `search_customer` to find a customer in our system (if you aren't sure who to look for, search for common names like "Acme", "Tech", or use a partner ID you found in step 2). Then, use `get_customer_details` to summarize their profile and recent quotes for me. 

### Step 4: Product & Quote Alignment
Use `get_products` to fetch our current inventory or service catalog. Recommend one of our active products that might be a good upsell for the customer you analyzed in Step 3.

### Step 5: Execute Actions (Invoice & Calendar)
Now, take action on that customer:
1. Use `create_invoice` to draft a "Consulting Services" invoice for $1,500.
2. Use `schedule_meeting` to book a 30-minute "Quarterly Business Review" with them for sometime next week.

### Step 6: Communications
Finally, use `send_email` to send a professional, friendly email to the customer. Inform them that their invoice has been drafted and that you've scheduled a placeholder for a QBR next week. 

Execute these steps chronologically. You may chain your tool calls if necessary, but keep me updated on your progress and provide a final summary of all the actions you took!
